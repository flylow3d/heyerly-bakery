# Heyerly's Bakery — concept website (spec build #9)

Concept/spec site for **Heyerly's Bakery**, 107 N Jefferson St (State Road 1),
Ossian, IN 46777 · (260) 622-4196. A classic small-town bakery, an Ossian
institution since 1931. Built by It's Already Done Web Co. from public
information — **the owners have not seen or approved it**; footer carries the
concept disclaimer. Style role in the portfolio: **vintage storybook cottage
bakery** (warm cream / dusty powder blue / butter yellow / berry; Grand Hotel
script + Bree Serif; scalloped-doily motif). See the master playbook:

## Facts used (researched July 2026) + WORDING RULES (obey strictly)

- **Name:** "Heyerly's Bakery" (apostrophe-s) — how the owners brand it on
  Facebook and how WANE/fwbusiness write it. (Directories drop the s.)
- **OWNERSHIP — critical:** founded 1931 by the Heyerly family; **SOLD Oct
  2019 to Gary & Kelly Short** (Wells County natives; son Justin joined). The
  four Heyerly brothers (Ron, Lynn, Stan, Galen — ~220 yrs combined) trained
  the Shorts ~2 months and handed over the recipes. **Do NOT imply the Heyerly
  family still bakes there.** Correct frame: "An Ossian institution since 1931
  — now cared for by the Short family, who kept the recipes." New owner quote:
  "We're not changing the doughnuts… those are iconic items."
- **Signature products** (all real, from reviews/news): fried cinnamon rolls
  (locals call them **"Ossian rolls"**; white-iced version), the **Texas donut**
  ("bigger than your head"), cream puffs ("out of this world"), **nut subs**
  (look filled but aren't — local specialty), sugar cream pie ("best around" —
  an Indiana classic), coconut cake ("very best ever"), blueberry cake donuts,
  glazed twists, apple fritters, apple strudel, bear claws, decorated sugar
  cookies (Valentine cookies praised), custom/photo cakes. ~400 dozen donuts on
  Saturdays. Free coffee + free donut holes at the counter.
- **NO PRICES anywhere** — none documented. Do NOT put a dollar figure on the
  site. Custom cakes: yes (incl. photo cakes) but **no lead time / no
  wedding-cake claim** — unconfirmed.
- **HOURS conflict** (opens 6 AM & closed Sunday agreed; close time reported
  5/7/9 PM). Site says: "Open early — Mon–Sat from 6 AM, closed Sundays. We
  bake fresh each morning and close when the day sells out, so come early."
  Do NOT assert a specific closing time.
- **Cash vs card UNCONFIRMED** (one directory says both) — say nothing about
  payment; the hero image's chalkboard was edited from "Cash Only" to
  "Welcome" for this reason (hero-bakery-v2.png; original had the claim baked in).
- **Do NOT name the 1931 founder** (Raymond R. Heyerly is a guess, unverified).
- Avoid absolute "unchanged since 1931" (post-2019 reviews are mixed) — use
  "the recipes the town grew up on."
- ~4.6–4.7★, ~548 Google reviews; Visit Wells County calls it "a Wells County
  staple and must-stop." Ossian: ~3,500, SR-1, ~10 mi south of Fort Wayne,
  Indiana Main Street community.
- Domains (RDAP 7/9/2026): heyerlybakery.com, heyerlysbakery.com,
  heyerlybakeryossian.com ALL AVAILABLE (exact-match, included tier).
